For Parents & Families

Your student's college experience matters.

Choosing whether to join Greek life can be an important decision in a student's college experience. We want families to have the information they need to understand fraternity life, the opportunities it can provide, and what membership in Theta Chi (THAY-tuh KYE) looks like at Ohio Northern University.

The Value of Fraternity

More than joining a social organization.

When approached with the right purpose, fraternity membership can provide students with opportunities to develop meaningful relationships, become involved in their campus and community, practice leadership, serve others, and build a network that continues beyond their undergraduate experience.

We also understand that families may want more than our perspective. Research examining the fraternity experience provides another way to understand the potential impact membership can have on students during and after college.

What the Research Shows

Independent research on the fraternity experience.

What That Means at Theta Chi

Brotherhood accompanied by responsibility.

The benefits associated with fraternity membership depend greatly on the experience a chapter creates for its members. At Theta Chi, our goal is to provide an environment where brotherhood is accompanied by responsibility, involvement, service, and opportunities for personal development.

Theta Chi has been part of the Ohio Northern University community since 1966. Our members come from different academic programs, backgrounds, interests, and experiences, but share the responsibilities that come with membership in our chapter.

“Alma Mater First and Theta Chi for Alma Mater.”

Theta Chi's maxim reflects an important expectation of membership. Our brothers are students first. The fraternity experience should complement a member's education while providing opportunities for growth outside the classroom.

Safety & Well-Being

Clear standards. Clear resources.

The safety and well-being of our members, pledges, guests, and community are fundamental responsibilities of chapter operations.

Our chapter operates within the policies and standards of Theta Chi Fraternity, Inc. and Ohio Northern University, as well as applicable federal, state, and local laws. This includes anti-hazing requirements established by law and the anti-hazing policies of both the International Fraternity and the University.

Ohio Northern University maintains zero tolerance for hazing within its community. Theta Chi Fraternity likewise prohibits hazing as part of the fraternal experience. Members and pledges are expected to follow the rules and policies of the Fraternity, our chapter, the University, and applicable law.

Hazing has no place in the fraternity experience.

Academics & Time Commitment

Fraternity membership introduces additional responsibilities, but those responsibilities should exist alongside, not in place of, a student's academic priorities.

Members balance classes with chapter activities, service, campus involvement, employment, athletics, and other commitments. Because schedules and individual academic demands vary, families with specific questions about current time commitments are encouraged to contact us directly.

Leadership & Personal Development

Members have opportunities to organize events, manage budgets and projects, lead committees, communicate with university officials and alumni, participate in chapter governance, and serve in elected leadership positions.

Leadership can also begin with accepting responsibility for a project, helping another brother, contributing to a committee, or becoming someone others know they can rely upon.

Service & the Assisting Hand

Service is an important part of the Theta Chi experience and reflects our Fraternity's tradition of extending an Assisting Hand to others.

These experiences reinforce the idea that fraternity membership carries responsibilities not only to fellow brothers, but also to the communities of which our members are a part.

Brotherhood & Belonging

College places students in a new environment filled with new people, responsibilities, and experiences. Brotherhood can provide a community within that larger experience.

The goal is not for every brother to be identical. It is to create a community where members can develop lasting relationships while contributing their own experiences, interests, and abilities to the chapter.

Beyond Graduation

A brotherhood that continues after college.

Membership in Theta Chi does not end at graduation. Our alumni remain part of the chapter's history and community, creating relationships between generations of brothers and providing continuity that no single undergraduate class could create on its own.

For today's members, that means joining a brotherhood whose relationships, traditions, mentorship, and opportunities for involvement can continue long after leaving Ohio Northern University.

Questions Are Welcome

Some questions are better answered through a conversation.

If your family has questions about safety, academics, costs, housing, time commitments, the pledge experience, or fraternity life at Ohio Northern University, we encourage you to contact us directly.

Asking a question does not commit your student to joining Theta Chi. We would rather families have the opportunity to ask questions and receive accurate information when making their decision.
